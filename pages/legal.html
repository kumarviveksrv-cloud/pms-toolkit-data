Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Effective: 1 June 2026

Quick Summary - the bits most people care about

  • We’re a learning platform for HR professionals - our job is to help you learn performance management.
  • The 16 calculators run inside your browser. Your uploaded data never reaches our servers.
  • The AI assistant + AI conversation simulator send your messages to Anthropic’s Claude API (deleted by them within 30 days, never used for training).
  • We collect minimal data about you - basic account info if you sign up, plus what’s needed to make payments work.
  • We don’t sell your data to anyone, ever.

1. Who we are

Virorah (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a performance management learning platform operated by Virorah Private Limited, registered in India.

For privacy questions, contact: info@virorah.com

2. What data we collect

Account data

If you sign up: your name, email, password (encrypted), and any profile info you choose to add.

Payment data

If you pay us: processed by our payment providers (Razorpay for India, Dodo Payments for international). We do not store your card details. Your payment provider’s privacy policy applies to that data.

Usage data (anonymous)

We collect basic anonymous usage stats (page views, feature usage) to understand how the platform is used. This is aggregated and does not identify individuals.

Browser-stored data (stays on your device)

If you upload a CSV to “Analyse Your Org”, that data lives in your browser’s local storage. We never receive it.

AI conversation data

If you use the AI Guide chat or AI conversation simulator, your messages are sent to Anthropic’s Claude API. See Section 5 for details.

3. What we don’t collect

  • We don’t track you across websites
  • We don’t sell your data
  • We don’t use third-party advertising trackers
  • We don’t fingerprint your device for marketing

4. Your CSV data (“Analyse Your Org” feature)

If you upload an employee CSV file to the “Analyse Your Org” feature:

  • The file is parsed and processed entirely in your browser
  • The data is stored in your browser’s local storage under the key pms_custom_org_v1
  • We do not have access to this data - it never leaves your browser
  • You can delete this data anytime via the “Clear Data” button or by clearing your browser data
  • This data is per-device and per-browser; switching devices means re-uploading

When you upload a CSV, you confirm via a one-time consent dialog that you are authorised to upload that data and have any required consent from the individuals it concerns.

5. AI features (AI Guide + AI Simulator + Debrief)

Virorah uses Anthropic’s Claude API to power our AI features. When you use these features:

  • Your messages (along with minimal context about which page you’re on) are sent to Anthropic’s API
  • Anthropic generates a response and sends it back
  • The response is shown to you in the browser
  • We do not log or store your conversation on Virorah’s servers

Anthropic’s data handling commitments to us (paid API customer):

  • Your messages are deleted by Anthropic within 30 days
  • Your messages are NOT used to train Anthropic’s AI models
  • Anthropic does not sell or share your data with third parties
  • Anthropic operates under a Data Processing Addendum aligned with GDPR

You can read Anthropic’s policies at anthropic.com/legal/privacy and anthropic.com/legal/data-processing-addendum.

Important: Please do not paste confidential information, real employee names, salary figures, or other personal data into the AI chat. The AI is a learning aid, not a confidential vault.

6. How we use the data we do collect

We use your account data to:

  • Provide you access to the platform
  • Send you account-related emails (login, password reset, important changes)
  • Process your payment if you subscribe
  • Respond to your support requests

We use anonymous usage data to:

  • Understand which features are used (so we build more of what works)
  • Identify and fix bugs
  • Plan our product roadmap

7. Who we share data with

We share minimal data with the following service providers, only as needed for the platform to work:

  • Anthropic - for AI processing (see Section 5)
  • Razorpay / Dodo Payments - for payment processing (only if you pay us)
  • Resend / our email provider - for transactional emails (only if you sign up)
  • Softr / Airtable - our platform infrastructure (account info only; no CSV data)
  • Cloudflare - hosting and security
  • Google Analytics or similar - anonymous usage stats (if applicable)

We do not share your data with advertisers, brokers, or any third party not listed above.

8. International data transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside India, including the United States and the European Union, where some of our service providers (such as Anthropic) operate.

For international transfers, we rely on:

  • Anthropic’s Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU-to-US transfers
  • Service providers’ published security and privacy commitments
  • Adequate safeguards as required by India’s DPDP Act 2023 and applicable laws

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access - request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction - ask us to fix inaccurate data
  • Deletion - ask us to delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-out - unsubscribe from non-essential communications
  • Portability - request your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@virorah.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

10. Data retention

  • Account data: retained as long as your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account closure.
  • Payment records: retained as long as required by Indian tax/financial law (typically 7 years).
  • Anonymous usage data: retained indefinitely in aggregated form.
  • AI conversations: not retained by us; retained by Anthropic for max 30 days per their policy.
  • CSV uploads: never reach us; stored only in your browser.

11. Security

We use industry-standard security practices:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data in transit
  • Secure password storage (hashed, never stored in plain text)
  • Limited access to systems on a need-to-know basis
  • Service providers with strong security postures (Anthropic, Cloudflare, Softr)

No system is 100% secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

12. Children

Virorah is intended for HR professionals and managers. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
