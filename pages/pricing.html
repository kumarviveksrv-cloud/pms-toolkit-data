Common questions
How do I cancel? ▼
To cancel your Virorah PMS subscription:

UPI payments: Open your UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.), tap your profile, go to Autopay or UPI Automatic Payments, find the Virorah mandate and cancel it.

Card payments: Log into your bank's mobile app or netbanking, go to Mandates or Standing Instructions, and cancel the Virorah recurring payment.

Important: No refunds are applicable for the current billing period. Your access continues until the end of your current billing month. Razorpay will send you a notification before each monthly charge so you are never surprised. We recommend cancelling at least 2 days before your renewal date.
What happens to my data if I cancel? ▼
Your uploaded org data is stored locally in your browser, it was never on our servers to begin with. When you cancel, your Pro access ends at the billing cycle. Your data remains in your browser until you clear it yourself.
What happens to my data after I upload it? ▼
Your data is processed entirely in your browser, nothing is ever sent to our servers. Your org data stays on your device, always. We built it this way deliberately, because HR data is sensitive and you deserve full control over it.
Can I try everything before paying? ▼
Yes. The Free tier has no time limit and no credit card required. You get 6 scenarios, 2 calculators (9-Box Grid and Bell Curve), the Spatial 9-Box, Forge Simulation Stages 1 & 2, 5 guides, and 3 case studies, all on a 750-employee sample dataset. Upgrade only when you're ready to use your real org data, bring a real situation to The Case Room, diagnose or build your team in FORGE, or earn your certification.
How does international payment work? ▼
International users pay via Dodo Payments, which handles global tax compliance, accepts cards across 150+ countries, and acts as the Merchant of Record so you never deal with currency conversion headaches. Indian users pay via Razorpay with UPI, cards, and net banking.
What exactly do the certifications include? ▼
Four levels: Fundamentals (30 questions, 45 min), Advanced (30 questions, 45 min), Practitioner (30 case-study judgment questions across 3 real HR scenarios, 90 min), and Portfolio (20 questions using live tools on real scenario data, 60 min). All four generate a PDF certificate with a unique verifiable credential ID.
Is this useful if I'm not currently in HR? ▼
Startup founders, people managers, MBA students, and business consultants all use Virorah PMS to understand how performance management actually works, and to have more credible conversations with their HR teams.
Will the price increase? ▼
Launch pricing is ₹999/month and $19/month globally. As the platform grows, pricing will increase, but existing subscribers will always be grandfathered at their original price for as long as they remain subscribed. Loyalty is rewarded, not penalised.
Is there a mobile app? ▼
Virorah PMS is a web-only platform, it works best on a desktop or laptop browser. There is no mobile app currently. This is a deliberate choice: the calculators, 3D visualisations, and data tools are designed for a full-screen experience that a phone screen simply cannot do justice to.
