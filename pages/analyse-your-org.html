Before you upload your data - read this

Hey 👋 - this is a one-time consent screen so you understand exactly how your organisation's data is handled. Please read carefully before continuing.

📍 Where your data goes

Your CSV file is processed entirely inside your browser. It is never sent to Virorah's servers, never stored on Airtable, and never transmitted to any third party. When you close this tab or clear your browser data, your file is gone from our side - because it never reached our side.

Want to verify? Open your browser's developer tools (press F12 → Network tab) and upload the file. You'll see zero outbound network requests carrying your data. Only the calculator code runs locally.
🔐 Where your data lives

The data sits in your browser's local storage (a per-device, per-browser sandbox) so the 16 calculators work across pages within your session. It's tied to your device - switching browsers or devices means re-uploading.

You can clear it any time using the "Clear Data" button on the upload page, or by clearing your browser data through your browser's settings.

⚖️ Your responsibilities

By clicking "I understand and agree" below, you confirm that:

  • You are authorised by your employer (or are the employer/owner) to upload this employee data to Virorah for analysis purposes.
  • You have any required consent from the individuals whose data is in this file, as required by the data protection laws applicable to you (such as India's DPDP Act 2023, the EU GDPR, or similar regulations).
  • You understand that Virorah is providing a software tool - similar to how Excel or a calculator is a tool. We do not store, see, or process your data on our servers.
  • You take responsibility for how you use the analysis output, and you will not hold Virorah liable for any decisions you make based on the analysis (such as compensation, ratings, terminations, or any other employment decisions).
  • You will not upload data you are not authorised to use - for example, data scraped, leaked, or otherwise obtained without proper authorisation.
🛡️ What Virorah commits to

We commit to keeping the upload feature browser-only. If we ever change this (for example, to add server-side AI analysis), we will: update this consent screen first, require fresh consent before any change applies to you, and email you in advance if we have your contact details.

Don't want to agree? You can still use all 16 calculators on our pre-built scenario data - no upload required. Just close this dialog and explore the Scenarios library instead.

By clicking "I understand and agree", you accept these terms. Your consent is recorded in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again.
