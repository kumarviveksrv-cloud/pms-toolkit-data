Why We Exist

Every HR framework in the world tells you what to do.
Almost none show you how it feels to do it.

You know the theory. You've read the books, sat through the webinars, memorised the frameworks. But when you actually walk into a calibration room, or hand someone a PIP, or defend a rating in front of your CHRO, the theory doesn't help.

Enterprise HR platforms like Workday, SAP and Darwinbox are built for HR operations at scale, and are excellent at what they do. But they sit behind a login you can only access when your employer buys them. You can't practice on them. You can't experiment. You can't fail safely. And none of them help you when a real situation lands on your desk today, or when your CEO asks how to build a genuinely high performing team and nobody ever gave you the playbook.

Virorah PMS is the missing layer, a Performance Intelligence platform where you can bring a real performance situation and get a complete action plan, build or diagnose a high performing team through FORGE, or run every performance decision on real data, in a real UI, without risking a real employee's career.

This is where HR practitioners go to get better, before it matters.

Inside FORGE

Diagnose what's broken. Or build it right from day one.

FORGE is where you work on team design directly, not a calculator applied to one decision, but the whole shape of how a team performs.

Forge Simulation puts you in charge of a team that's already broken. You inherit it exactly as it stands and make real decisions across six stages, eighteen calls in total, and an AI generates the consequences of each one. At the end you get an HPT score out of 100, a number that tells you exactly how close you came to building something extraordinary, and how far off.

Forge Your Org runs the same rigour on your actual team. Score your organisation against five diagnostic conditions grounded in real, documented cases, and walk away with a 90-day playbook for what to fix first.

The Foundry helps you build a genuinely extraordinary team, either from scratch or by intentionally redesigning one that already exists, across six conditions grounded in real cases from Skunk Works to SpaceX. Work through each stage and walk away with a tangible artifact for your team, six in total, combined into a single Operating Kit.
